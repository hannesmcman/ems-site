## Services

# Oil Paintings

Oil paint is my first love, though I’ve dabbled in many forms of two-dimensional fine art mediums. I accept commissions on a case-by-case basis. The amount I charge varies widely depending on complexity, size, and the materials needed. If you’d like to commission a piece, my preference is that you have a reference photo or two in mind, or , if not, that you’re open to my artistic interpretation of your concept.

[Send me an email](mailto:{{site.data.settings.email}}?subject=Oil Painting Inquiry)

# Trade Work

I’ve been a house painter in some form for the last five years. I’m self-employed, and currently most of my working time is spent doing trade work. I typically charge $30-$35 per hour, but I’m willing to be flexible depending on the person or situation. For reference, it takes me 3-4 hours total to paint two coats in a bedroom that’s twelve by twelve feet and that has several windows. I’m almost always accepting new jobs in the twin cities, so if you’re looking for someone to paint your bathroom, guest room, entryway, or all three, shoot me an email and we can talk about possible timelines.

[Send me an email](mailto:{{site.data.settings.email}}?subject=Trade Work Inquiry)

# Murals

I also accept mural commissions. I’m pretty flexible when it comes to subject matter and style, so as long as the surface is paintable, I’m willing to do it. Again, email me if you’ve got an idea.

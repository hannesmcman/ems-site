# about

Hi! My name is Em, I’m based out of Minneapolis, and I’ve almost always got paint on my hands. I don’t remember a time when I wasn’t creating in some form; color and light are two of the things I’m most fascinated by as an artist and as a human, and bringing things to life on a canvas is endlessly gratifying. Oil paint is my first love, though I’ve dabbled in many forms of two-dimensional fine art mediums.
